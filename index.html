🎯 How to use it with the Adrian Strategy

🟢 Gold line above USD line

Gold bullish bias. Only take BUY setups — 5m pullback into support, 1m bullish rejection candle. Ignore any sell signal that forms.

🔴 Gold line below USD line

Gold bearish bias. Only take SELL setups — 5m rally into resistance, 1m bearish rejection candle. Ignore any buy signal that forms.

🟡 Lines touching / gap too small

Do nothing. The market has no clear direction. The 1m trigger is likely a trap. Wait for the lines to separate by at least the SecureGap value.

⚠️
The meter is a filter, not a trigger. Do not wait for the meter to change before entering your 1m candle — you will be late. Use it only to decide whether you are allowed to take longs or shorts. The entry remains the close of the 1m rejection candle at the 5m zone, inside 11:00–15:00 or 16:00–20:00 EAT.
